# Attune

**Music in tune with your life.**

Attune is a web music player that adapts to you: it can match your weather and mood, sync with friends, match your walking pace, turn songs into art, and remember where you heard them. It plays your own music files, runs entirely in the browser, and can be installed as an app.

> 🚧 Early stage. See [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Planned features

- 🌦️ **Mood & sky**: the queue and theme follow your weather and time of day
- 🎧 **Listen together**: share a link and hear the same song in sync
- 🎚️ **Smart local library**: BPM and key detection with DJ-style crossfades
- 🏃 **Pace match**: songs that match your walking or running tempo
- 🎨 **Song art**: a unique visual for every track
- 🗺️ **Memory map**: where and when you played each song

## Built with

TypeScript · Svelte 5 · Vite · Web Audio API · IndexedDB · PWA

## Privacy

Your music never leaves your device. No account, no server.
